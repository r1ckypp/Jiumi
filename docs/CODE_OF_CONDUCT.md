# Community guidelines

**English** · [Simplified Chinese](zh-CN/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

These guidelines apply in project issues, reviews and discussions. Welcome people working in either language and at every level of experience. Explain the behavior you observed, offer concrete alternatives and give authors credit for their work.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks and publishing private information are unacceptable. If a conversation crosses a boundary, ask a repository maintainer privately for help. Include a link and the action you are requesting, and share only the details needed to understand the incident. Maintainers may moderate content or limit participation to keep project spaces usable.
